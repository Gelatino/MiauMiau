A night to gather our ghosts and party with them.

Oct 31 Villa K, Berlin 6PM — Midnight
↓ follow the path ↓

This year we make a party, based on that old Mexican tradition where the alive and those who left us sooner than we thought can gather together for one more night.

We create a communal altar where we all contribute: food, drinks, a picture of our dear ones that passed away. We celebrate them all together, the Mexican way, pan de muerto, chocolate, tequila, all to honor them.

Summon your ghosts. Follow the cempasuchil path, the bridge that connects this world to the other one, and celebrate with us: the life, and the death.

The Communal Altar

Together we build an altar to honor those who have passed. This is not decoration, it is a gathering place for remembrance. Bring a photograph of someone you want to honor, bring a food they loved when alive, bring a candle, bring flowers, bring whatever feels right.

Every offering sits side by side, creating a shared space where the living and the dead meet. We make this altar together, adding to it throughout the night, a collective gesture of love and memory.
